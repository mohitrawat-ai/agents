Ingren RCA · access overview

How Ingren connects to your AWS account

When an incident fires, Ingren's RCA agent investigates it the way an engineer would: it reads your metrics, logs, and resource configuration, and writes up the root cause. To read that telemetry it needs read-only access to your AWS account. This page explains what that access is and how you stay in control of it. It is an overview for an early conversation, not a setup guide.

How access works

A read-only role in your account, assumed by ours

This is the standard AWS pattern for giving a vendor scoped access, called a cross-account IAM role. You never hand over credentials, and we never store any.

1

You create a role

An IAM role lives in your account, with read-only permissions attached. You create it once in the AWS console. We provide the exact steps.

2

The role trusts only Ingren

The role can be assumed only by Ingren's AWS account, and only with a secret ID we share with you privately. No one else can use it.

3

The agent reads through it

During an investigation, the agent assumes the role, reads the telemetry it needs, and writes its findings outside your account.

Why not an API key? A key is a credential that can leak and must be rotated. A role gives out short-lived access that AWS itself controls, and every use of it shows up in your own audit trail (CloudTrail). It is the mechanism AWS recommends for third-party access.

Your controls

You stay in control the whole time

ControlHow it works
Read-only by constructionThe role's permissions contain only describe, list, and read actions. AWS enforces this, not a promise from us.
You see every accessEach time the agent assumes the role, it is logged in your CloudTrail. You can audit exactly what was read and when.
Scoped to what's neededThe permissions cover the services in your incident path. We don't ask for account-wide admin or blanket access.
Revoke in one clickDelete the role in the IAM console and access ends immediately. No process on our side, nothing to uninstall.

What happens next

Setup comes after we've mapped your architecture

Nothing on this page requires action today. The sequence when we start:

1

Architecture walkthrough

A short call where we map your services, so the access request covers what your incidents actually touch and nothing more.

2

Tailored setup guide

We send a step-by-step guide with the exact policies for your stack, plus your private secret ID. About 10 minutes in the console.

3

Verify and go live

We confirm access with a read-only test call and connect the agent to your alerting. From then on, incidents get investigated automatically.

The exact permissions depend on your architecture and are finalized together in step 1. The structure never changes: a read-only role in your account, a private secret, full audit visibility, revocable by you at any time.
